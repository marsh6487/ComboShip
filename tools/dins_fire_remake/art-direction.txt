Din's Fire remake POC1 — original flame art

The retained flame_source.png was generated for this POC using the built-in
image-generation tool. It is an original texture inspired by the supplied clip,
not an extracted texture from the remake. No supplied video pixels are shipped.

Prompt:
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production-ready grayscale intensity
texture for a real-time magical fire shader in a Zelda-style game. Create a
square seamless tile of turbulent, flowing flame wisps and ragged curling flame
tongues, viewed completely flat as a texture map. Strict grayscale only: pitch
black empty space, smoky dark-gray thinner flame, medium-gray broad sheets,
bright white fine hot filaments inside tongues. A chaotic but graceful flowing
pattern with long diagonally and laterally curling, shredded flame ribbons,
tiny broken wisps, several substantial irregular black transparent gaps
between flame sheets. About 55 percent black negative space. Different
thicknesses and scales of detail. It must read as luminous living FIRE when
colorized gold/orange in-engine, not clouds, lava cracks, marble, lightning,
water, tribal ornament, or a drawing of a complete flame. Pattern fills the
whole square with even visual density and smoothly tiles on all four edges,
both horizontally and vertically; no obvious centered subject, no bottom
ground line, no flames growing out of the bottom border, no gradient vignette.
No colored pixels, no text, no labels, no border, no UI, no objects, no
screenshot, no preview sphere. The black itself is the zero-intensity
transparency mask. Return only this single texture.

The generated source is retained unchanged. Native encoding resamples it to
256 x 256 with grayscale intensity in RGBA. Both logical sampler dimensions
remain 64 x 64. No artistic seam repair or replacement was applied.

At 256px the measured wrap-edge differences (mean absolute intensity change,
0..255) are X=9.04 and Y=11.46, compared with adjacent-pixel means X=12.16 and
Y=12.68. This supports periodic sampling but does not establish in-game
material correctness. Approximately 39.1 percent of pixels are below 16;
7.3 percent are above 160. Actual color/opacity depends on the native combiner.

sparkTex is a small analytical glint, generated by the exact encoder formula.
It combines a white core, soft halo, cross rays and shorter diagonal rays,
with an empty border. The renderer colors it mint-green or warm orange.
